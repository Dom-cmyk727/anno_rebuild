# slots visited per Advance from a circular cursor (cursor 0 at start), visited in ascending slot order; ships in the listed slots each have

# HAND DERIVATION (n = 100, dtick = 5: k = 10, so the cursor window 0..9 covers slots 1..5 only every 10th Advance, all five in the SAME Advance, order 1,2,3,4,5):
#   quota_first (the rejected rule), quota 4: slots 1..4 are served in every one of the 100 cycles, slot 5 never -> 100 100 100 100 0.
#   fifo_reserve, adversary replace_served (a served ship is re-commanded at once with a new youngest ticket): cycle 0 serves 1..4 and defers 5; cycle 1: ship 1..3 are
#   admitted (one older ticket, 5, is still pending and quota remains for it), 4 is deferred (1 older < 1 remaining is false) and 5 is admitted; the deferral then alternates
#   between 4 and 5 every cycle -> 100 100 100 50 50, and no ticket waits more than 2 cycles (20 Advances).
admission quota_first 100 5 1,2,3,4,5 4 1000 replace_served | counts 100 100 100 100 0
admission fifo_reserve 100 5 1,2,3,4,5 4 1000 replace_served | counts 100 100 100 50 50
