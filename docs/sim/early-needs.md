7. **Arithmetic (hand-derived) versus elapsed time.** *Arithmetic only:* one trip credits 768 raw grass (3 t); the sheep farm turns 768 into **52 wool in 9 steps** (8 x 87 -> 6 each, then 72 raw -> 4), leaving 1 raw; 52 wool make 28 cloth in 5 weaver steps. A weaver's carrier takes goods only from a source holding >= 1 t (256) of them. *Elapsed time is a separate question and these numbers say nothing about sustainable supply.* A lower bound for the **first pickup of wool** (assumptions: the first eligible farm step is at tick 500 and every later 500-tick step sends one more animal, one figure per eligible step as vector F-fresh/`send`; grass always within reach; zero walking time; raw limits nothing after the first trips): raw grass reaches the farm only when a trip ends (step C).
