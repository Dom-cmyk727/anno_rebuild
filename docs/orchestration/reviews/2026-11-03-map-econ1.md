`src/sim/src/harvest.cpp:284–286`, with
`src/sim/src/invariants.cpp:72–79`.** Normal harvest completion releases its
claim and reaches Done before ending the figure. The public

new harvest worker must retain a valid, reloadable world.

control pass immediately before ending; the counter releases and expiry is set;
then validity/reload fail, plus both enhanced claim-cleanup assertions. The
corrected exploratory probe has **36 passing control assertions and 6 failed

its reload result was discarded; the retained `end-probe-correct-seams.out`
