## 3. Findings and decisions

### F1 [P2] — two of the branch's verdict guards were dropped, against step 2's rule

Step 2 said: take main's `tools/test`, unless the branch's version holds a fix main lacks; then port that fix with a
test. Only the depth review's F6 was ported. The branch's version (`9448a3af`) also had two tests that main's lacks:
- `test_every_skip_phrasing_in_the_sources_has_a_known_class` scans every `"skipping…"` and `"skipped…"` literal under
  `src/` and requires a known class.
- `test_the_scenario_phrasings_are_required_assets`.

This review ran that scan with the merged classifier over the merged sources. It classes 8 of 34 phrasings as `other`.
Four of them report a missing original file and belong in `required-asset`:
- `play_test.cpp`: `skipping: no scenario files` and `skipping: no endless scenario`;
- `building_delta_test.cpp`: `skipping: 06_1024_heavy_256.sze is not in this game directory` and
  `skipping: Single_01.sze is not in this game directory`.

**The impact:**
- Main's verdict fails a gate on `other`, so no gate passes wrongly.
- The stated reason is wrong, though.
- Under a no-asset policy such as CI's, these would fail instead of being reported as missing assets.
- The scan would also have caught CI fix 7's two platform phrasings before CI did.

Main's cut-log check needs no port: `run_selected.py` always passes a CTest inventory.

### F2 [P2] — River Q5 had no bar decision, so `main` would inherit a failing game-file test

W4-OPENING-1-CLOSE item 1 reserved the per-island bar for the orchestrator, and nobody had set it. Since the projection
fix, `D_256x256_0045_5B` scores as follows (other four islands and all windows together pass):

| Shore | Windows passed | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | 16 of 33 | 48.48 % |
| 16.5 | 17 of 35 | 48.57 % |
| before the projection fix | 41 windows | 63.41 % |

The island's river raster itself changed with the sprites' corrected positions: 7 rivers and 10 ponds became 6 rivers
and 14 ponds. The flow is solved on a different river.

**Decision D1:**
- **`D_256x256_0045_5B` gets a per-island floor of 0.45.** That is one window below the measured value.
- **Unchanged:**
  - every other island's 0.55;
  - the 0.75 share over all windows, and the minimum of 150 windows;
  - the ratio checks.
- **How the exception is written:**
  - as a per-island field of the test's island table, not as a skip;
  - with a comment that gives the measured values and links this review.
- **The 0.55 target for D** comes back as an acceptance item of the river rework (the queued river feedback).
- **If the depth package's shore of 26.5 puts D below 0.45,** DEPTH-1-CLOSE reports the value and stops for a decision.
  It does not move the floor itself.

**Reason:** the drop follows a correct projection fix that changed the river input, not a defect in the shading. A
floor keeps a regression guard and lets `main`'s game-file gates pass. The enhanced water's look stays the owner's to
judge, and the owner may override this.
