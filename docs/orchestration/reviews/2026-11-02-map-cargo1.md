expeditions and no cities. Seats 0 and 2 sail, found their own warehouses,
unload their remaining cargo and build HANDWERK and PIONIER through public
commands. Site planning relocates a ship on a separate trial world only; the
recorded world sails normally, with bounded movement and untouched cargo to
arrival. No grant or relocation appears in that executed path.

The production unload path resolves current ids and authority, validates range
and contents, computes the amount before writes, and credits/debits that exact
amount. The stock helper never clamps an over-capacity store down or wraps a
sum. Target lookup uses existing indexes, not a world scan. Requests address
one hold in whole tons; stored balances remain exact 1/256-ton units.
Per-goods wide conservation tests, capacity/fraction/limit cases, competing
ships, fresh target lookup, codecs, persistence and cargo-dependent later
sailing segments exercise the public command path.

The original's rounding loss and malformed-state arithmetic are documented
separately from the rebuild's conservation and authority adaptations. Own
warehouses are the implemented scope; loading, foreign trade, cannon handling
and automatic routes remain separate work. Additional data-defined ship and
warehouse types are tested. Warehouse eligibility and dock geometry are saved
and hashed in CargoRules; ship-class eligibility retains the documented
founding default (BEWOHNTYPE 113).

Rival warehouse/build/ship attempts receive the expected authority refusals.
Reload after founding, after unloading and at both boundaries matches ledger,
hash and save bytes of the uninterrupted opening.

Keep these scope limits explicit; none blocks this accepted opening slice:

1. Both arrival positions are already in docking range. This real-file canary
   does not prove an extra move-then-unload leg. Synthetic tests do exercise
   live position/range and unloading while a movement segment is active.
2. The opening case does not assert shared visibility is enabled. The loader
   policy is tested elsewhere; its authority refusals alone are not proof of
   that precondition. Live integration must inspect its actual policy state.
3. The ledger measures between-command stock/gold changes, reporting zero in
   this run; it does not assert those terms must always be zero. That is a
   limit on future regression detection, not permission to grant resources.
   Immediate transfer and building-payment checks remain independent of those
   observed terms. This is not a complex-economy accounting/performance proof.
4. The credit-without-debit mutant has cascading failures in the sequential
   guard, so its whole-run failure list is not an isolated debit-only result.
   Debit/report/per-goods conservation assertions are present, and the
   limited-credit/request-debit counterexample is separately discriminated.
5. Ship class is not configurable by mods yet. Warehouse/range rules and
   additional types are data-driven; do not claim general capability-based
   mod support. Future shared eligibility must remain deterministic and
   identifiable across navigation, founding and cargo.
6. Stream **124** refuses 123 saves/replays. Command wire version remains 1
   under the existing append-only native-tag policy. Neither that unchanged
   wire number nor these Mac results establishes a general network-compatibility
   claim. Unresolved cargo OQ-1..3 and original-runtime captures remain named.
7. Windows/MSVC, Linux/WSL warnings/sanitizers, renderer/GPU validation and
   live-window integration of this Mac candidate remain pending. Two exercised
   seats do not establish five-player multiplayer or late-game scale.
