## 2. Rules for this plan

- **Base:** every packet branches from `refs/candidates/cand12` (later from `main` once candidate 12 has landed),
  never from an older `main`.
- **One PC, two executors:** each executor works in its own worktree under `E:\anno1503-worktrees\` with its own
  build folder. Only one of them runs GPU or long tests at a time: before a GPU or long run, say so in the report
  line and check the PC is quiet (WSL included). A memory refusal during the other lane's build is rerun alone,
  not counted as a failure.
- **Files:** the render lane owns `src/render/**`, `docs/render/**`, their tests, and the picture loader files
  `src/app/{src,include/anno/app}/{loader,scene_picture}.*` (W2b). The UI lane owns `src/app/src/viewer.cpp`,
  `viewer.hpp`, `play.cpp`, `building_delta.cpp`, `placement_ghost.cpp`, `src/app/src/ui*`, `data/ui/**`. Any
  other shared `src/app` file is touched only after asking the orchestrator, who serialises it. Each lane may add
  lines to `src/app/CMakeLists.txt` and `docs/open-questions.md`; the orchestrator resolves those at stacking.
- **Numbers:** OQ-775..784 render lane, OQ-795..799 then OQ-900..919 UI lane (OQ-800..899 are the Mac lane's,
  `mac-lane-2026-11-17.md`). No state number (139 is the last). The next free header extension tag is 3; asking
  first.
- **Size:** one concern per packet, about 2-4 hours. A packet runs the build, the tests of what it changed,
  clang-format and the WSL warning check for touched C++, and hosted CI on its pushed head. No reviewer subagents
  (the orchestrator reviews). Report in REPORTS.md (at most about 60 lines) and by message to the orchestrator.
- **Ponytail:** on full in every session and subagent. It never removes a test or evidence that `CLAUDE.md` or the
  evidence contract requires.
- **Heavy runs per bundle only,** on this PC while the Mac is away (CPU, GPU and RAM): the full game-file suite with
  GPU tests, once per bundle, by the orchestrator.
