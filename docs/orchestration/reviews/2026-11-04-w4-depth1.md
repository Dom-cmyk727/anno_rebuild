## 3. Owner decisions

**1. The water level.** The trace measured the original's sea plane at **≈ 26.5 `HEIGHT`** (26.25–28.25 by zoom and
view rotation). At the same world points, the original's coverage matches `--shore 26.5`:

| Height band | Original covered | `--shore 26.5` covered |
|---|---|---|
| below 24 | 98–99 % | 96 % |
| 24–26 | 73 % | 96 % |
| 26–28 | 21 % | 24 % |
| above 28 | ≤ 2 % | ≤ 4 % |

The default 16.5 (your H1 choice) leaves dry sand where the original shows water. But at 26.5 our coarse sea-region
rule (8 × 8-tile chunks, land classifier 19) shows as stepped water edges on wide beaches. The original draws its sea
on every pixel whose cached height lies below the plane, so its edge follows the terrain.

**Recommendation:**
- keep 16.5 for now;
- release W4-SHORE-2 (queued below), which draws the sea against the plane per pixel;
- then compare 26.5 against the GOG capture and choose the default.

**2. The `--depth` switch.** Recommendation: keep it, default on. On is the original's behavior. Off is the former
picture, a comparison and fallback whose digests are pinned.
