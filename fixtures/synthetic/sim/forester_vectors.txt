# claims faithful|enhanced ops... -> bit owner   (E2.0 rewrite, docs/sim/forestry.md E-16; derived by hand from the D facts)
#   W+ select-and-claim (refused while the bit is set); W- W's own cut arrival/abort (faithful: unconditional clear;
#   enhanced: only the owner); P placement and C clearing KEEP bit 29 (AnnoWelt 0x100824a7 `and 0x3fffffff` clears
#   bits 30-31 only; the clear arm 0x10082f05 keeps bits 25-31); W~ enhanced owner revalidation releases its tile.
#   THE DISCRIMINATING CASE: claim then placement leaves the faithful claim set (the withdrawn mask reading gives 0).

#   clearing keeps it too; the legal worker unclaim clears it (the control that must stay green under the mutant).

#   placement does not free the tile for a second worker: B is refused, A still holds it.

#   regression only: ends at 0 under BOTH mask readings (does not discriminate on its own).

#   faithful one-bit model: a clear by another worker at the tile is unconditional (an accepted loaded state only).
