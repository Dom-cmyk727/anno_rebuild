# Units: raw 1/256 ton ("units"); a request is in whole tons. Goods ids are the original's: 26 NAHRUNG (food),
# 41 WERKZEUG (tools), 42 HOLZ (wood). `goods_count` is the number of goods a city stock holds (default 56 = 0x38).
# `cap` is the city's capacity of every goods (default 12800 = 50 t). A hold written `0 0` is an emptied hold.
# `req <ship> <hold index> <goods> <tons> -> ok <transferred units> | reject <REASON>`; REASON is an engine Reject
# enumerator name. `orig <return> <hold after> <stock after>` is what the ORIGINAL's arithmetic (AnnoWelt.dll 0x100ac360
# unload arm, 32-bit register semantics, hold amount a u16) does for the FIRST request of the case; it is evidence of the
# difference, never an expectation of the engine. `endhold <ship> goods:units ...` and `endstock <goods> <units>` are
# the state after all requests of the case (goods not listed must be unchanged from the `stock` lines).
